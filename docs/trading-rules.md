# Trading Rules & Risk Management Baseline

## Core Rules

1. **Maximum Daily Loss**: Limit daily net realized loss to a maximum threshold ($500 default).
2. **Maximum Daily Trades**: Limit total trade executions per day to avoid overtrading (max 10 trades/day).
3. **Risk Per Trade**: Maximum risk per single trade position setup must not exceed 2% of portfolio value.
4. **Position Sizing**: No single position should exceed 20% of net equity.
5. **Options Risk Rules**:
   - Never hold expiring option long positions through market close unless explicitly planned.
   - Respect assignment and exercise risk on short legs.

## Preferred Setups

- **Momentum Breakouts**: High volume consolidation breakouts with defined stop-loss.
- **Trend Continuation**: Pullbacks to key moving averages (20 EMA, 50 SMA).
- **Mean Reversion**: Oversold/overbought extremes at major support/resistance.

## Emotional Constraints

- **No Revenge Trading**: After 2 consecutive losses, pause trading for at least 1 hour.
- **Strict Stop-Loss Execution**: Never move a stop-loss further away after entering a position.
